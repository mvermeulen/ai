THE ART OF THE PERFECT STEEP

Chapter 1: The Morning Ritual
The sun began its slow ascent over the mist-covered hills of the valley. Inside the small stone cottage, Elias was busy preparing his first cup of tea. For Elias, this was not merely a drink; it was a silent ceremony that anchored his day. 

He believed that the secret to a great tea lay in the patience of the brewer. "Water," he often whispered to no one in particular, "must never be rushed. It must be respected." He watched the steam curl into the cold morning air, smelling the earthy aroma of dried leaves.

Technical Specifications for Brewing:
1. Water Temperature: For green tea, ensure the water is between 175°F and 185°F (80°C - 85°C). Boiling water will scorch the delicate leaves and create a bitter taste.
2. Steeping Time: Allow the leaves to rest in the water for exactly three minutes. 
3. Equipment: Use a ceramic or glass teapot to maintain a consistent temperature throughout the process.

The Conversation:
Suddenly, there was a sharp knock at the door. It was Clara, the local messenger.
"Elias!" she shouted, breathless. "The shipment from the coast has arrived earlier than expected. We need your help at the docks."
Elias sighed, looking at his half-steeped tea. "Patience is a virtue, Clara, but I suppose duty calls. Give me a moment to grab my coat."

Philosophical Note:
In a world that moves at the speed of light, there is profound power in choosing to slow down. Whether it is making tea or translating a story, the quality of the result is always a reflection of the care put into the process.
